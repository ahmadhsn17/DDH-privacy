Privacy Policy

Your cards, payments, installments, loans and renewal dates — managed locally on your device.

Effective date: November 19, 2026
Application: Due Dates Hub (DDH) for Android

This Privacy Policy explains how Due Dates Hub ("DDH", "the app") handles information in the current offline-first version. The app helps individuals track credit-card statement amounts and payments, loan and card installment schedules, annual fees, and document expiration or renewal dates. DDH does not issue loans, process payments, connect to bank accounts, or request online banking credentials.

1. Information you choose to enter

You can enter labels for cards or lenders, optional last four digits of cards, financial amounts and due dates, installment and repayment details, document type and holder labels, expiration dates, and optional notes. This information may be personal or financially sensitive.

The app does not ask for full payment-card numbers, CVV/security codes, full passport or ID numbers, bank passwords, or scanned identity documents. Please do not put this information into optional text fields.

2. Storage and data transfers

The app stores your entered records in local application storage on your Android device. The current version does not operate a developer-run user account, cloud database, advertising network, or analytics service. Its normal tracking functions do not intentionally transmit your records to a developer-controlled server.

The operating system and any device-level backup settings you enable are separate from DDH and may affect whether app data is backed up or restored by the device platform.

3. Notifications

With your permission, DDH schedules local Android reminders based on the dates you enter. Notifications may display card or loan labels, due dates, amounts, or document descriptions on your phone, including potentially on its lock screen. You can turn reminders off in the app or revoke notification permission in Android settings.

4. Exporting, importing and sharing backups

You can export your data to a JSON file or import a previously saved backup. Exported JSON backups are not encrypted. If you choose to save, send, or upload them using another application or cloud provider, those services handle the file under their own privacy practices. Keep backup files private and remove copies you no longer need.

5. Sharing with third parties

In the current version, DDH does not sell, rent, or intentionally send your entered records to advertisers, analytics providers, or banking services. Any external sharing of a backup is initiated by you through your device's sharing controls.

6. Retention, correction and deletion

Records remain on your device until you change or delete them, restore another backup, clear the application's storage, or uninstall it. Device backup or restoration features may affect this behavior. Files you exported outside the app must be removed separately from the locations where you saved them. Since DDH does not provide user accounts or store records on a developer server, there is no developer-held account database to delete.

7. Security

Android application storage provides operating-system access controls, but the app's exported JSON backups are not encrypted. Avoid entering highly sensitive identifiers or banking credentials. Use your device's screen lock and keep your operating system updated.

8. Children

Due Dates Hub is intended for adults managing personal financial obligations and renewals. It is not designed for children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when DDH's functionality, services, or data handling change. The latest version will appear on this page with its updated effective date.
